### Current plan (2 full-body days, A/B, about 60 min each)

Order: warm-up, power (while fresh), strength, core, conditioning, cool-down.

- A: med ball rotational throw 4 × 4. Goblet squat 3 × 6–8, half-kneeling landmine press 3 × 6–8 per side, seated cable row 3 × 8. Pallof press, dead bug. Air bike 6 × 20/40.
- B: KB swing 5 × 6. Romanian deadlift 3 × 6–8, goblet reverse lunge 3 × 6, face pull 3 × 12. Side plank. Rower 5 × 30/30, jump rope 2 × 2 min.
- Shared warm-up: jump rope, 90/90, couch stretch, band pull-aparts. Cool-down: couch stretch, doorway chest stretch.
- Pressing only on A (landmine, scalable per side). Boxing covers the rest of the pushing.
- Progression: start light, 2 reps in reserve. When all sets reach the top of the range, go one step heavier. Later phase: drop to 4–5 × 3–5 on squat, RDL and press once loads are solid.
- RSI handling: right arm sets the reps on single-arm work, left matches. Neutral grips, goblet holds, straps on RDL. Every grip- or arm-heavy move has a swap.
