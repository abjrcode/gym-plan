### Current plan (2 full-body days, A/B, about 60 min each)

Order: warm-up, power (while fresh), strength, core, conditioning, cool-down.

- A: med ball rotational throw 3 × 4. Goblet squat 3 × 6–10, single-arm low-to-high cable press 3 × 6–10 per side, light lateral raise 2 × 12–15 (trial, swap: band pull-aparts), seated cable row 3 × 8–10, cable pull-through 2 × 10–12. Dead bug. Air bike 6 × 20/40.
- B: KB swing 3 × 6. Romanian deadlift 3 × 6–10, goblet reverse lunge 3 × 6–8, machine chest press 2 × 10–12 (trial, swap: single-arm cable press), seated cable row 2 × 8–10, face pull 3 × 12–15. Pallof press 2 × 8 (swap: dead bug). Rower 5 × 30/30, exercise bike 4 × 20/20 (swap: jump rope).
- Shared warm-up: easy bike 3 min (swap: jump rope 6 × 20/10), 90/90, couch stretch, band pull-aparts. Cool-down: couch stretch, doorway chest stretch.
- Pressing: single-arm cable on A, light machine chest press on trial on B. Boxing adds more pushing.
- Trials: one new upper-body move per session, so a flare has a clear cause. Drop it if ache passes 3/10 or lasts to the next day. Lat pulldown is next, once both trials stay calm for two sessions.
- Progression: start light, 2 reps in reserve. When all sets reach the top of the range, go one step heavier. Keep the in-app weights guide (`index.html`) in step with these rules. Later phase: drop to 4–5 × 3–5 on squat, RDL and press once loads are solid.
- RSI handling: right arm sets the reps on single-arm work, left matches. Neutral grips, goblet holds, straps on RDL. Every grip- or arm-heavy move has a swap.
