Glute plan

Pick a weight that leaves 2 reps in the tank. When every set feels that easy, go a little heavier next time. Feel it in your glutes, not your lower back. Tap a set when you finish it to start the rest clock.

Train 1, 2 or 3 times a week: just do the next day in line, A → B → C → A. The dot marks what’s next. Leave a rest day between sessions.
